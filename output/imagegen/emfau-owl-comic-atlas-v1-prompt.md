Eigene Zeichnung; transparente Ausgabe unverändert in `emfau-site/dist/assets/emfau-owl-comic-atlas-v1.png` gespeichert. Die vorherige Eule bleibt erhalten.

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset: ONE transparent raster animation texture atlas for a small friendly owl on a tasteful portfolio website. Landscape canvas, strict 3 columns by 2 rows, components well separated with generous transparent padding. No text or grid.
Reference image: ONLY the friendly expressive cartoon character aesthetic of the owl in the screenshot. Create an ORIGINAL distinct owl, do not reproduce its exact design or any screenshot UI.
Style: polished hand-painted storybook comic illustration, broad simple scalloped feather shapes rather than individually realistic feathers. Round gentle face, tiny beak, plump soft belly, softly rounded small ear tufts, curious welcoming expression, large eyes. Calm muted chestnut and terracotta, oatmeal cream, warm tan, blue-grey iris; restrained sage leaves. Much friendlier and simpler than a naturalistic owl. Avoid bright red, neon yellow, intimidating brows, photorealism, vector/SVG aesthetics.
Exactly seven distinct cutout components in this layout:
TOP LEFT: complete frontal HEAD ONLY, no body. Cream scalloped facial disk, tiny dark beak, two round blue-grey eyes WITHOUT pupils or highlights (uniform blue-grey eye centers). Symmetrical, face looks welcoming.
TOP CENTER: HEADLESS BODY ONLY, plump rounded torso with folded small wings, simple cream chest with broad tan feather marks. No tail, no feet, no branch. Extend chest behind neck so a head can overlap smoothly.
TOP RIGHT: a thin gently curved horizontal twig with a few small sage leaves and TWO owl feet gripping its CENTER. No body. Do not make leaves oversized.
BOTTOM LEFT: one isolated round dark pupil with a small white catchlight.
BOTTOM CENTER: TWO separate closed eyelid patches side by side, left and right, each a cream feather-textured oval with a gentle curved closed-eye line. Plenty of gap between the two patches. Neither patch includes an eye or face.
BOTTOM RIGHT: ONLY detached tail fan: three or five soft rounded chestnut feather tips with light tan bands, vertically pointing down, narrow attachment at top, subtle fan at bottom, no body or feet.
Genuine alpha transparency around EVERY component, no checkerboard or color fill. Keep all components within their own cells, no overlapping. Coherent shading and smooth clean cutout edges.

## Kontrolle

1536 × 1024 Pixel, RGBA, transparente Leerflächen. Tatsächliche Ausschnitte manuell an die Bildausgabe angepasst. Kopf, Augenlider, Körper, Ast und Schwanz in der zusammengesetzten Browseransicht geprüft.
