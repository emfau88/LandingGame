# Eule: Animationstextur

Erstellt mit dem eingebauten ImageGen-Werkzeug, Bearbeitung der Vorlage `emfau-owl-concept-v1.png`, transparenter Hintergrund. Originalausgabe unverändert als `emfau-site/dist/assets/emfau-owl-atlas-v1.png` gespeichert. Die Laufzeit verwendet Bildausschnitte, ohne SVG oder Spine-Runtime.

## Prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit.
Asset type: ONE production transparent texture atlas for a canvas animated owl, six isolated components arranged on a strict 3-column by 2-row regular grid, each component entirely inside its own equal cell with generous transparent margins. Landscape 3:2 sheet. No labels or visible grid.
Input image 1: edit target and character identity reference. Preserve its beautiful painted feather detail, taupe brown, ivory, slate blue eyes, folded wings, natural twig with sage leaves.
Top left cell: ONLY the complete owl head, frontal symmetric pose, feather ears and facial heart, beak, round slate-blue irises but NO black pupils and NO eye highlights. Paint the eye centers uniformly pale blue, not holes. Head cutout ends below chin in a soft feather fringe. No body.
Top middle cell: ONLY the headless complete owl body, folded wings, chest, and long tail below, no feet, no branch. Extend chest fully behind the head overlap, softly rounded shoulders, no empty hole where head was.
Top right cell: ONLY the horizontal gently curved branch, muted sage leaves and both owl feet gripping near its center; no other owl parts. A thin elegant branch, same style.
Bottom left cell: ONLY one round black pupil with a tiny white catchlight, clean circular silhouette, no iris.
Bottom middle cell: ONLY a closed LEFT eyelid, an ivory feather-textured oval patch wide enough to fully cover the circular blue eye, with a dark curved closed-eye crease across its center; no surrounding face.
Bottom right cell: ONLY a matching closed RIGHT eyelid, same feather textured oval patch, mirrored counterpart.
All six cells must be isolated without bleed or overlap. Real alpha transparency throughout empty areas. Components are separate cutouts, not a finished assembled owl. No text, no background scene, no checkered pattern, no shadows floating outside components. Polished coherent raster illustration, not SVG, not vector.

## Kontrolle

PNG: 1536 × 1024 Pixel, RGBA, echte transparente Leerflächen. Die tatsächlichen Ausschnitte wurden anhand der Ausgabe gewählt, weil die Platzierung kein exaktes regelmäßiges Raster bildet.
