# Eulenentwurf v1

Erstellt mit dem eingebauten ImageGen-Tool. Transparenter Rasterentwurf für eine spätere interaktive Figur. Die Datei ist eine zusammengefügte Illustration, keine separierte Animationsvorlage und noch kein Spine-Rig. Die Website wurde nicht geändert.

## Verwendeter Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: original raster character artwork, first visual concept for a small interactive owl on a professional German freelance web designer's website. This is the character art, not a website mockup and not an animation sprite sheet.
Primary request: a carefully illustrated owl perched on a slender gently curved natural branch with a few small leaves, complete owl and branch isolated on a genuinely transparent background. Original character design, do not reproduce any existing mascot or copyrighted demo artwork.
Style/medium: polished hand-painted 2D game-character illustration with convincing soft volume, delicate feather texture and clean readable contours. Raster-painted, not vector, not SVG, not flat geometric icon art, not photorealistic and not glossy 3D plastic. Charming and observant but restrained enough for an elegant portfolio website.
Subject: front-facing owl in a neutral resting pose, balanced approximately symmetrical face, large expressive open eyes with separate clear dark pupils and tiny natural highlights, rounded yet anatomically credible feathered head, small beak, folded wings, compact body, visible feet gripping the branch and a little tail. Clear separation of head, face, eyes, wing edges, body and branch in the painting, useful as a visual reference for later layered character rigging. No implication that this single image is already rigged.
Color palette: subdued warm grey-brown and taupe feathers, ivory face and chest, natural muted brown wood, a few sage leaves, subtle desaturated slate-blue eye irises. Harmonize with warm off-white #f8f7f4, dark ink #282d2a and restrained blue #365f91.
Composition: whole figure and branch centered, owl is the visual focus and occupies roughly two thirds of the artwork height, branch modestly extends to both sides, all feathers, feet, tail, branch tips and leaves fully in frame, comfortable transparent margins. Readable at about 180 pixels tall. Soft consistent diffuse lighting, shading confined to the subject, no background scenery, no floor, no background shadow, no glow.
Constraints: no text, letters, labels, watermark, logo, gradients in the background, stars, sparkles or extra props. Truly transparent background.
